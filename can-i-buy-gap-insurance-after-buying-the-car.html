Can I buy GAP insurance after buying the car?

Home / Guides / Can I buy GAP insurance after buying the car?

Quick answer

Yes. You can usually buy standalone GAP insurance after you have the car, but only before a loss, and only inside the time limit that provider publishes. There is no single UK legal deadline. On pages checked on 18 November 2026, ALA's Back to Invoice Plus cover is for vehicles owned for less than 180 days (365 days if your motor insurer's new-car replacement applies while the car is under 12 months), MotorEasy's return to invoice cover is for a dealer purchase within the last six months, and Click4Gap says cover is for a vehicle bought within the last 180 days. A dealer who sells GAP with the car is under a different rule: ICOBS 6A.1 stops that firm concluding its sale until two clear days after it gave you set information, unless you start the purchase yourself the next day.

Updated 18 November 2026

The windows below are copied from named UK providers' own pages. They are not a legal maximum, and another provider can set a shorter or longer one. Read the wording on the quote you are offered.

What you need

  • The date you took delivery. The windows above run from the purchase or delivery, which can differ from the day you ordered the car.
  • The purchase invoice. ALA's AmTrust wording says you will be asked for the purchase invoice if you claim. MotorEasy's claim steps ask for the original purchase invoice or sales receipt.
  • The finance agreement, if there is one. The AmTrust wording requires any finance agreement to be in your name, and the amount originally financed to be no more than the purchase price on the invoice.
  • Comprehensive motor insurance. Both that AmTrust wording and MotorEasy require a comprehensive policy. On those wordings, third party cover is not enough.
  • The V5C, and an MOT if the car needs one. MotorEasy asks for the V5C logbook when you claim, plus a current MOT certificate if one is required. Have them ready for a quote as well.
  • Age, mileage and existing damage. Check them against the table. MotorEasy excludes incidents before the policy start date, so a scrape or write-off that has already happened is not something a new policy will pick up.

If you buy and then change your mind, cancellation is a separate step. The cooling-off rules are in cancelling GAP insurance and refunds. How a later claim is put together is in how to make a GAP insurance claim.

The dealer two-clear-day rule

ICOBS 6A.1 applies to a firm that sells a GAP contract in connection with the sale of a vehicle by that firm, or by a connected person. A connected person includes an introducer or appointed representative, or a firm with a relevant business relationship. The handbook says there is a sufficient connection if the GAP contract is sold with other goods and services, for example a credit agreement.

Before that sale is concluded, the firm must give you, in a durable medium: the total premium separate from other prices; the significant features, benefits, exclusions and limitations; that GAP contracts are sold by other distributors; the policy duration; whether the contract is optional or compulsory; when the firm can conclude it; and the date that information was provided. The information has to be drawn to your attention.

The firm then cannot conclude the contract until at least two clear days have passed, unless you initiate the conclusion the day after, consent to the shorter wait, and confirm that you understand the usual restriction. A worked calendar for that count is in the FCA rules guide. The FCA's 2018 evaluation found that some people, given the time, decided not to buy at all. The price reason for using the pause is in dealer vs online GAP insurance.

That rule does not set how long a standalone provider will keep quoting. It does require the firm selling GAP with the vehicle to say whether that GAP contract is optional or compulsory, and to show the premium as its own figure.

If the invoice window has closed

Missing a 90-day, 180-day or six-month window does not always mean no policy exists. ALA's cover guide says agreed value can be bought at any time. MotorEasy offers return to value after six months, or from the day of a private purchase. Both are based on a value when the GAP policy starts, so they do not put you back to the original invoice. Return to invoice, vehicle replacement and agreed value shows the difference on one set of numbers.

A purchase nine days later, and a request 14 months later

James collects a car in Cardiff on 2 March. The dealer gives him the GAP information that day and offers to add £540 to the invoice. He says no. On 11 March he buys a standalone policy with a £20,000 claim limit for £250. Both prices are an illustration, not a price survey. Nine days is inside the windows ALA, MotorEasy and Click4Gap publish for an invoice-style policy. The sequence is a normal use of the deferral: he heard the offer, he waited, he bought elsewhere.

Ruth bought her car 14 months ago. After a car-park scrape she asks for GAP, hoping to cover a future write-off and the scratch. The scratch is already outside a new policy: MotorEasy excludes incidents before the start date. Fourteen months is also outside the invoice windows in the table. She could still ask about agreed value or return to value, which those providers say can start later, but neither product turns the existing scratch into a claim.

Common questions

Can I buy GAP insurance after buying the car?
Yes, if it is before a loss and inside the limit that provider publishes. Checked on 18 November 2026: ALA's Back to Invoice Plus page allows less than 180 days of ownership, or less than 365 days if new-car replacement applies while the car is under 12 months. MotorEasy's return to invoice page allows a dealer purchase within the last six months. Click4Gap's homepage allows a purchase within the last 180 days. There is no single legal number of days.
Does the FCA two-clear-day rule stop me buying GAP from someone else?
No. ICOBS 6A.1 applies when a firm sells a GAP contract in connection with the sale of a vehicle by that firm, or by a connected person. That firm cannot conclude its own GAP sale until at least two clear days after it gave you the prescribed information, unless you start the purchase the next day, consent to the shorter wait, and confirm you understand the usual restriction. A different provider you contact yourself is a separate sale.
What if I have missed the return to invoice window?
You may still be offered a different type of policy. ALA's cover guide says agreed value GAP can be bought at any time, including for a private purchase or after other time limits have passed. MotorEasy says return to value is for a private purchase, or a dealer purchase more than six months ago. Those policies top up towards a value at the start of the GAP cover, not the original invoice. Read that wording before you buy.
Can I buy GAP after the car has already been written off or damaged?
No. Cover is not backdated to a loss you already know about. MotorEasy's return to invoice page excludes incidents before the policy start date. A cooling-off cancellation does not create cover for an incident that happened before the policy started.
