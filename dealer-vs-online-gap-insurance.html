Dealer vs online GAP insurance

Home / Guides / Dealer vs online GAP insurance

A dealer policy and an online policy can insure the same shortfall. They are not sold in the same way. Dealer GAP is an add-on at the vehicle sale, and FCA rules slow that sale down. Online GAP is a standalone purchase you make yourself. The FCA found that add-on prices could be twice the price of broadly equivalent standalone cover.

Same job, different shop

Dealer GAP is introduced while you are buying the car, often by the seller or a firm connected to the seller. Online, or any other standalone purchase, is a policy you go and find. The FCA's market study found a point-of-sale advantage: in the evidence set out in CP14/29, 83% of people who bought add-on GAP did not consider another policy, compared with 9% of people who bought standalone GAP.

That is why the price gap mattered. The same consultation said add-on GAP prices could be significantly higher than standalone prices, and could be twice as expensive for broadly equivalent cover. A later FCA evaluation in July 2018 found that shopping around had more than doubled after the new rules, but that the move from add-on to standalone sales was smaller than the FCA had expected.

What the dealer is required to do

Where ICOBS 6A.1 applies, the firm has to give you set information on paper or another durable medium before the GAP contract is concluded. That includes the total premium on its own, the main features and unusual exclusions, the fact that other distributors sell GAP, how long the policy lasts, and whether it is optional. The firm then has to wait at least two clear days, unless you yourself ask to go ahead from the next day and confirm that you understand the usual wait.

The full mechanics, including a dated example, are in the FCA rules guide. Use the wait. It exists so the decision is not made in the same conversation as the car.

What changes when you buy later, online

A separate insurer you contact yourself is not concluding the dealer's add-on sale, so the two-clear-day rule is not what slows that purchase down. The provider will still set its own conditions: how recently you bought the car, the car's age and mileage, and that it has not already been damaged or stolen. There is no single legal number of days. See whether you can buy GAP after the car.

Online is not automatically the better policy. A cheaper premium with a claim limit below your finance balance, or a wording that excludes your type of agreement, is not a saving. Check the firm on the Financial Services Register.

An example of the price difference

Example only

£610 in the showroom and £290 from a standalone provider

A dealer offers return-to-invoice cover with a £25,000 claim limit for £610, to be added to the car invoice. The next week a standalone provider quotes £290 for a three-year policy with the same claim limit and the same basis of settlement.

The £320 gap is an illustration, not a survey of prices this year. It is in the direction of the FCA's finding that add-on cover could cost about twice as much as broadly equivalent standalone cover. If the online policy had a £10,000 claim limit, or excluded cars over three years old, the two quotes would not be equivalent and the cheaper one could be the wrong one.

These pound figures are an illustration so you can see the arithmetic. They are not a quote, a market average, or a claim paid by a named insurer.

Compare these lines, not the logo

  • Basis of settlement: invoice, finance balance, lease termination charge, or a new replacement.
  • Claim limit, and whether it is enough for your own arithmetic.
  • Term, matched to the finance or lease rather than to a round number of years.
  • Motor excess: included up to a cap, or left with you.
  • Negative equity rolled in from a previous car, dealer-fitted extras, and the vehicle's age and mileage.
  • Who is paid: you, the finance company, or the leasing company.

Common questions

Is dealer GAP illegal?
No. Dealers and finance firms can still sell it if they follow the FCA's information, deferral and fair value rules. The 2024 pause was agreed with firms that could not show fair value. It was not a ban on the product.
Do I have to buy the dealer's policy to get the finance?
No. A firm selling GAP with a vehicle must tell you whether the contract is optional or compulsory, and must tell you that other distributors sell GAP. If someone says the finance will not go ahead unless you take their policy, ask them to put that in the paperwork.
Can I buy online on the day I collect the car?
The deferral binds the firm selling GAP in connection with the vehicle sale. It does not stop you approaching a separate provider. You still cannot buy cover for a crash or theft that has already happened.
Will a dealer policy and an online policy pay the same amount?
Only if the wording matches. Compare the basis of settlement, the claim limit, the term, what happens to the motor excess, and any exclusion for negative equity brought over from a previous car. The marketing name is not enough.
