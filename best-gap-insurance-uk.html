Best GAP insurance in the UK

Home / Guides / Best GAP insurance in the UK

Updated 18 November 2026

The best GAP insurance is the policy that pays the shortfall you would actually face, on a car it accepts, at a price you have compared like for like. The cheapest premium is not automatically that policy. This page does not award its own ratings. It checks a published test against the article, then the comparisons that decide which quote fits.

The checks that decide the best policy

  • Maximum claim. The policy cannot pay more than its limit. Check it against the shortfall you could face, not an average.
  • Excess cover. In the Auto Express test this ranged from £250 to £1,000.
  • Eligibility. Providers set limits on vehicle age, mileage, value and how long ago you bought the car. ALA's Back to Invoice Plus page, checked on 18 November 2026, says the vehicle must be less than 10 years old and owned for less than 180 days, or less than 365 days if new-car replacement applies while the car is under 12 months. That page also says there is no upper mileage limit. The AmTrust wording ALA publishes, dated 1 September 2026, says fewer than 100,000 miles. ALA says different scheme providers use different wordings, so use the one on the quote. The purchase windows are collected in can I buy GAP after buying the car.
  • How the payout is calculated. Some policies pay the higher of the invoice price or the finance settlement; others pay only one. Read the claim calculation, not just the policy name.
  • Claim time limits. How long you have after the total loss to make a GAP claim.
  • Who underwrites it. The provider should be on the FCA register, and the documents should name the insurer.
  • Cancelling and transferring. Your 14-day right to cancel, and what happens if you sell the car.

Example only

Why the cheapest quote is not always the best

Tom has a car on PCP that cost £28,000, with a £24,000 settlement figure today. Policy A costs £140 and has a £15,000 maximum claim. Policy B costs £185 and has a £25,000 maximum claim.

If the car were written off and the motor insurer paid £10,000, Tom would be £14,000 short on the finance and £18,000 short on the invoice price. Policy A could only ever pay £15,000. Policy B could cover the whole gap on an invoice-based claim. For Tom, the £45 difference buys real cover.

These pound figures are an illustration so you can see the arithmetic. They are not a quote, a market average, or a claim paid by a named insurer.

How to choose and compare GAP insurance

There is no official cheapest GAP insurance in the UK, and a comparison only means something when the policies insure the same shortfall. In the Auto Express test above, eight providers priced one return-to-invoice policy: a nearly new Ford Puma on hire purchase, a £15,000 claim limit, three years. The published premiums ran from £107.35 to £205.44. That is a real spread on one brief. It is not a league table for every car, and it is not a promise that Moneymaxim, or anyone else, is the cheapest quote you will get.

The FCA's competition work found that GAP sold with the car could be twice as expensive as broadly equivalent cover bought separately. That is a reason to compare a dealer price with standalone quotes. It is not a reason to buy the lowest number without reading the claim limit. Whether the cover is worth it depends on the shortfall you could not pay yourself. Dealer and online GAP are the same job sold in different places.

  1. Name the figure you need insured: the invoice price, the finance settlement, the lease charge, or a replacement car. The cover types do not all pay at once.
  2. Set the claim limit against that shortfall. A cheaper policy that stops at £15,000 does not beat a dearer one if your gap could be larger. The example above is the arithmetic.
  3. Check age, mileage, vehicle value, and how long ago you bought or collected the car. Limits differ. Buying after you have the car sets out windows that named providers publish.
  4. Compare excess cover, and read what is excluded. Repairs, refused motor claims and sums above the claim limit are the usual gaps.
  5. Get at least two quotes for the same cover type, claim limit and term. Published price ranges and a rough calculator are in how much GAP insurance costs.
  6. Check the seller on the FCA register and read the policy before you pay. If a dealer is selling GAP with the car, ICOBS 6A.1 gives you time to do this.

Common questions

Which is the best GAP insurance provider?
There is no single answer, because policies pay out on different figures and accept different cars. In Auto Express's 2026 test of eight online providers, Moneymaxim was named Best Buy for the lowest quote and an easy site, Direct Gap was suggested for higher excess cover, and MotorEasy for paying monthly or bundling. Those are Auto Express's verdicts on one car and one cover type.
Is dealer GAP insurance ever the best option?
It can be if the price and cover match or beat standalone quotes, but the evidence is that dealer add-on GAP has often cost more. Ask for the details in writing and compare before you agree.
What should I check before buying GAP insurance?
The cover type, the maximum claim, the policy length, how much of your motor excess it pays, the age, mileage and value limits, the time limit for buying and for claiming, who underwrites it, and the cancellation terms.
Does the cheapest GAP policy pay out less?
Not necessarily, but it can if the claim limit is lower, the cover type is narrower or the excess cover is smaller. In Auto Express's 2026 test the lowest quote and the highest were for the same return-to-invoice brief, so price and cover were not the same thing. Compare like with like before treating the lowest price as the best deal. There is no official UK cheapest policy.
