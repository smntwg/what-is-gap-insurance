Best GAP insurance in the UK

Home / Guides / Best GAP insurance in the UK

The best GAP insurance is the cheapest policy that pays out on the figure you would actually be short, for a car the policy accepts. That depends on whether you need invoice, finance, lease or replacement cover, and on the claim limit. This page does not award its own ratings. It sets out what a published like-for-like test found, then the checks that decide which policy fits you.

The checks that decide the best policy

  • Maximum claim. The policy cannot pay more than its limit. Check it against the shortfall you could face, not an average.
  • Excess cover. In the Auto Express test this ranged from £250 to £1,000.
  • Eligibility. Providers set limits on vehicle age, mileage, value and how long ago you bought the car. ALA, for example, says its Back to Invoice Plus cover is for cars up to 10 years old bought within the last 180 days.
  • How the payout is calculated. Some policies pay the higher of the invoice price or the finance settlement; others pay only one. Read the claim calculation, not just the policy name.
  • Claim time limits. How long you have after the total loss to make a GAP claim.
  • Who underwrites it. The provider should be on the FCA register, and the documents should name the insurer.
  • Cancelling and transferring. Your 14-day right to cancel, and what happens if you sell the car.

Example only

Why the cheapest quote is not always the best

Tom has a car on PCP that cost £28,000, with a £24,000 settlement figure today. Policy A costs £140 and has a £15,000 maximum claim. Policy B costs £185 and has a £25,000 maximum claim.

If the car were written off and the motor insurer paid £10,000, Tom would be £14,000 short on the finance and £18,000 short on the invoice price. Policy A could only ever pay £15,000. Policy B could cover the whole gap on an invoice-based claim. For Tom, the £45 difference buys real cover.

These pound figures are an illustration so you can see the arithmetic. They are not a quote, a market average, or a claim paid by a named insurer.

Comparing quotes

Once you know the cover type and claim limit you need, get at least two quotes for exactly that, then compare excess cover, eligibility and claim terms. The GAP insurance cost guide has the published price ranges and a rough calculator. If a dealer is offering GAP with the car, the FCA rules give you time to do this.

Common questions

Which is the best GAP insurance provider?
There is no single answer, because policies pay out on different figures and accept different cars. In Auto Express's 2026 test of eight online providers, Moneymaxim was named Best Buy for the lowest quote and an easy site, Direct Gap was suggested for higher excess cover, and MotorEasy for paying monthly or bundling. Those are Auto Express's verdicts on one car and one cover type.
Is dealer GAP insurance ever the best option?
It can be if the price and cover match or beat standalone quotes, but the evidence is that dealer add-on GAP has often cost more. Ask for the details in writing and compare before you agree.
What should I check before buying GAP insurance?
The cover type, the maximum claim, the policy length, how much of your motor excess it pays, the age, mileage and value limits, the time limit for buying and for claiming, who underwrites it, and the cancellation terms.
Does the cheapest GAP policy pay out less?
Not necessarily, but it can if the claim limit is lower, the cover type is narrower or the excess cover is smaller. Compare like with like before treating the lowest price as the best deal.
