GAP insurance for vans

Home / Guides / GAP insurance for vans

A van can leave the same sort of gap as a car if it is on finance or contract hire and the motor insurer pays market value. The extra questions are whether the GAP policy accepts vans, whether your motor insurance covers the way you use the van, and whether the insured figure is the price before VAT or the price after it.

Business use has to be right before GAP matters

The ABI says that if you use a vehicle as part of your employment you need business use on the motor policy. Carrying your own tools to jobs is not the same as carrying paying passengers, which needs hire and reward cover. If the motor insurer refuses a claim because the use was wrong, GAP does not step in. Get the motor policy right first. Citizens Advice's claim guidance on market value applies to a van write-off in the same way as a car: you can challenge a low offer with evidence of similar vehicles.

A worked example with VAT

Example only

A £3,700 finance shortfall on the net price, not the VAT-inclusive one

A VAT-registered decorator in Coventry buys a panel van. The invoice is £28,000 plus VAT of £5,600, so £33,600 gross. The business reclaims the VAT and finances £28,000 on hire purchase. Two years later the van is written off. The motor insurer pays a market value of £19,400 to the lender. The HP settlement figure is £23,100.

The finance shortfall is £3,700. A GAP policy that insures the net amount financed is concerned with that £3,700. Someone who subtracts £19,400 from the £33,600 gross invoice gets £14,200 and overstates the gap, because the £5,600 of VAT was reclaimed and was not part of the finance. These figures are an illustration. Ask the provider which invoice number they use, and keep the VAT invoice with the policy.

Tools in the back, a roof rack and the signwriting are not automatically part of the van's insured value. GAP on the vehicle does not pay for stolen tools unless a separate possession cover says so. That cover, where it exists, is usually on the commercial motor policy and has its own limit.

These pound figures are an illustration so you can see the arithmetic. They are not a quote, a market average, or a claim paid by a named insurer.

Contract hire vans

A van on contract hire is the lease problem, not the invoice problem. The leasing company receives the market value, and the hire contract bills an early termination charge. Your exposure is the difference. MoneyHelper's contract hire guide describes that early-exit cost, and the leased cars guide works a car example you can mirror. Mileage and damage charges are still usually outside GAP.

Eligibility is in the policy, not in a national rule

There is no FCA rule that says every GAP policy covers vans up to a set weight. Providers choose. A crew cab, a refrigerated body, a tipper or a gross weight above the policy limit can be declined even though the vehicle is still an ordinary working van. Conversions and modifications need to be on the proposal. If you are buying the van on hire purchase rather than hiring it, the settlement arithmetic is the one in the HP guide, using the lender's figure rather than a guess from the monthly rental.

Used vans raise the same age and mileage questions as used cars. A van that has already done a high mileage may fail a provider's limit even if the finance balance is uncomfortable. Ask before you treat cover as a given.

Common questions

Do I have to tell the motor insurer the van is used for work?
Yes, if you use it for work. The ABI says that using a vehicle as part of your employment needs business use on the motor policy, and that the wrong use can invalidate the policy. A refused motor claim means there is no GAP payout.
Can I buy a car GAP policy for a van?
Only if that policy lists vans as eligible. A proposal that describes the vehicle as a car, when it is a van, is a claims problem. Do not assume the word vehicle on a car website includes a panel van.
Does GAP pay for a hire van while mine is off the road?
No. A courtesy or hire van is a benefit on the motor or commercial policy, with its own daily limit. GAP pays a shortfall after a total loss or theft settlement, not a temporary replacement.
What if I am not VAT registered?
You cannot reclaim the VAT, so the price you paid and the amount you financed may both be the gross figure. Use that gross figure on the proposal and in any claim, and do not mix it with a net price from a different document.

GAP insurance for leased cars

On contract hire you never own the car. The gap is the early termination charge minus the insurance money.

GAP insurance for hire purchase

HP has no balloon. The shortfall is usually smaller than PCP, and largest early in the agreement.

GAP insurance for used cars

A used car can still leave a gap if you overpaid or you owe more than the insurer will pay.

What GAP insurance does not cover

Repairs, refused motor claims, arrears and sums above the claim limit are the usual gaps in the cover.
