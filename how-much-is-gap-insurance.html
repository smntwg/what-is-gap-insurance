How much is GAP insurance?

Home / Guides / How much is GAP insurance?

Bought online, three years of GAP insurance on a mainstream car usually costs somewhere between about £100 and £300. In Auto Express's 2026 test, eight online quotes for the same three-year return-to-invoice cover on a nearly new Ford Puma ranged from £107.35 to £205.44. The FCA's figure for the whole market, including dealer sales, was higher: an average premium of £330 in 2022. Your price depends on the car, the claim limit, the length of cover and where you buy it.

Why the same cover costs different amounts

  • The car and the claim limit. A dearer car can leave a bigger shortfall, so the maximum claim and the price go up.
  • The type of cover. Return to invoice, finance GAP, contract hire GAP and vehicle replacement pay out on different figures. The cover types are compared here.
  • The length of the policy. The FCA says the average GAP policy runs around 36 months. Two to four years is common.
  • Extras. Excess cover varied from £250 to £1,000 in the Auto Express test, and some providers add hire car contributions.
  • Where you buy. The FCA found add-on GAP sold with a car could be twice as expensive as broadly equivalent standalone cover. In 2023 it told GAP insurers that commission could be in excess of 70p for every £1 of premium.
  • Tax. Insurance Premium Tax is 12% at the standard rate, but a higher 20% rate applies to certain insurance sold with some motor vehicles.
  • How you pay. Instalments can cost more than paying upfront.

What that works out at per month

These are our own divisions of the published figures, so you can compare a monthly instalment offer. The 2026 Auto Express quotes, spread over 36 months, come to about £2.98 to £5.71 a month. The RAC and MSE range of £100 to £300 over three years is about £2.78 to £8.33 a month. The FCA's 2022 average of £330 over about 36 months is about £9.17 a month. If a monthly figure you are offered is well above these, ask for the total price and compare it.

Example only

A dealer quote against an online quote

Priya is buying a £19,000 car on finance. The dealer offers three years of GAP for £420, which would be added to the finance. She asks for the policy details in writing and, during the two-day wait the dealer has to allow, gets two online quotes for return to invoice with the same £19,000 claim limit: £165 and £190.

The cheaper online policy saves her £255, before any interest she would have paid on the dealer premium. She checks that the cover type, claim limit, excess cover and claim time limit match before choosing, because a cheaper policy that pays out on a lower figure is not a saving.

These pound figures are an illustration so you can see the arithmetic. They are not a quote, a market average, or a claim paid by a named insurer.

How to get a fair price

  1. Work out what you need covered: the invoice price, the finance settlement, the lease charges or a brand new replacement.
  2. Ask any dealer for the GAP price and documents in writing. The FCA's deferral rules mean it cannot complete the sale straight away.
  3. Get at least two standalone quotes for the same cover type, claim limit and term.
  4. Compare the excess cover, eligibility limits and claim time limit, not just the price.
  5. Check the provider on the FCA register and read the policy before you pay. You normally have 14 days to cancel.

Common questions

How much is GAP insurance per month?
Spread over three years, Auto Express's 2026 online quotes of £107.35 to £205.44 work out at roughly £3 to £5.71 a month. The FCA's 2022 market average of £330, over its typical policy length of about 36 months, is about £9.17 a month. Paying by instalments can cost more than paying upfront.
Is GAP insurance cheaper online than at the dealer?
Usually, according to the evidence published so far. The FCA's competition work found add-on GAP sold with a car could be twice as expensive as broadly equivalent standalone cover. One online provider, GAPinsurance.co.uk, says the 280 customers who reported a dealer quote between August 2025 and July 2026 had been quoted £416.90 on average, against its own average premium of £250.47, though the policies were not necessarily identical.
What makes GAP insurance more expensive?
A more expensive car and a higher claim limit, a longer policy, wider cover such as vehicle replacement rather than return to invoice, more excess cover, and buying from the dealer. Insurance sold with some motor vehicles can also carry the higher 20% rate of Insurance Premium Tax rather than the standard 12%.
Is the FCA's £330 average a fair price to aim for?
It is a market average for 2022, across standalone and add-on policies, before the FCA's 2024 intervention pushed firms to improve value. It is a useful upper reference point, not a target. Like-for-like online quotes published in 2026 were well below it.
Do I pay GAP insurance every year?
Most policies are bought once for a fixed term, often two to four years, and paid upfront or in instalments. The FCA has said the average GAP policy lasts around 36 months.
