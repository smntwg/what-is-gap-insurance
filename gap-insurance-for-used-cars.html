GAP insurance for used cars

Home / Guides / GAP insurance for used cars

GAP insurance on a used car is worth buying when you paid more than the car will be worth at a total loss, or you still owe more on finance than the insurer will pay. It is much less useful when you bought close to the going price and you own the car outright. Used cars usually fall in value more slowly than a brand new one, but a financed used car can still leave a shortfall in the first year or two.

Two used cars, two different answers

The ABI makes the general point that any vehicle depreciates, and that a motor insurer pays market value rather than the price you paid. On a used car the gap between those figures is often narrower than on a new one, because the steepest fall has already happened. Finance changes the picture, because the settlement figure can still sit above market value. MoneyHelper explains how an early HP settlement is calculated if you need the lender's number.

Example only

£1,700 on finance, and a £500 gap that may not be worth insuring

Adeel buys a four-year-old SUV from a dealer in Leicester for £18,450. He pays a £500 deposit and finances the rest on hire purchase. Ten months later it is written off. The market value is £15,200. The settlement figure is £16,900. The finance shortfall is £1,700. Against the invoice, the market value is £3,250 below the price he paid. A finance policy looks at the £1,700. A return-to-invoice policy looks at the £3,250. He does not get both.

The same model, bought privately for £14,900 because that matched similar adverts, is assessed at £14,400 eight months later. There is no finance. The gap to the price paid is £500. A policy that cost more than a few hundred pounds would be an expensive way to move a £500 risk. Adeel in the first case has a clearer reason to look at cover than this cash buyer does.

These pound figures are an illustration so you can see the arithmetic. They are not a quote, a market average, or a claim paid by a named insurer.

Age, mileage and where you bought it

Providers set their own maximum age and mileage at the start of the policy. There is no single UK cutoff in FCA rules. A car that one provider refuses may be eligible with another, and a car outside every provider's limits is simply not a GAP candidate. Ask before you pay a deposit to a dealer on the assumption that cover will be available next week.

A previous insurance write-off can also be excluded. GOV.UK's guide to repaired written-off vehicles explains categories A, B, S and N, and says a previously written-off vehicle can cost more to insure. Tell the motor insurer. A GAP provider may refuse category S or N cars entirely. That history is covered again in the stolen and written-off guide.

Invoice, finance, or neither

If you own the car outright and the price you paid is close to market value, cash buyers often decline GAP for good reason. If the finance balance is the only figure above market value, finance cover matches the debt and return to invoice may be paying for a larger number than you need. If you bought well above the going rate because the car was rare or you were in a hurry, return to invoice is the product that looks at that overpayment, provided the policy accepts the invoice.

Buying cover after you have the car

Used-car buyers often want a few days to think, which is exactly what the dealer deferral is for. Providers then set their own window for a later purchase. There is no law that says 180 days, or any other single number. The conditions are in can I buy GAP insurance after buying the car.

Common questions

Is return to invoice available on a used car?
Often yes, if you have a dealer invoice and the car's age and mileage are inside that provider's limits. A private sale receipt may not meet the policy definition of an invoice. Check before you assume the price you paid a neighbour will be the insured figure.
Do used cars depreciate enough to matter?
They can, if you paid over the odds or financed most of the price. They often do not, if you bought at the market price for cash. There is no single percentage that applies to every used car, so use your own invoice and a cautious value.
Will GAP pay if I bought from a private seller?
Only if that policy accepts private sales. Many are written for purchases from VAT-registered dealers. If private sales are excluded, the policy will not pay just because you have a bank transfer as proof of the price.
Does a warranty replace GAP?
No. A warranty contributes to mechanical repairs. GAP pays a shortfall after a total loss or theft settlement. A car that is repaired under a warranty is not a GAP claim.
