GAP insurance for PCP

Home / Guides / GAP insurance for PCP

On a personal contract purchase, GAP insurance is there for the difference between the motor insurer's payout and the finance settlement figure. That figure is often higher than the monthly payments you have left, because the optional final balloon is still in the agreement. The guaranteed future value at the end of a healthy PCP is not the same thing as GAP.

What you owe is not the same as the monthly payments left

MoneyHelper's PCP guide sets out the three parts: a deposit, monthly payments, and an optional balloon if you want to own the car. You do not own the car during the agreement. You are responsible for insuring it. If it is written off or stolen, the agreement does not disappear. The lender is paid from the insurance money, and you remain liable for any shortfall.

MoneyHelper also notes that dealers may offer GAP with a PCP, and that you should check you are getting a fair deal. The FCA's 2024 fair value concerns are the regulatory version of that warning. They are not a reason to ignore a real shortfall if your own numbers are uncomfortable.

Finance GAP and return to invoice are different sizes

Finance GAP aims at the settlement figure. Return to invoice aims at the price you paid. On a PCP those numbers diverge as soon as you have paid a deposit and some instalments, and they diverge again because the balloon may be inside the settlement but is not an extra on top of the invoice. If you only need the debt cleared so you can start another PCP, finance cover matches that debt. If you want the original purchase price back, return to invoice is the closer product. The comparison of those bases uses one set of figures so you can see the difference.

A worked PCP example

Example only

A £31,250 car and a £4,800 finance shortfall after theft

Priya takes a PCP on a car with a cash price of £31,250. Her deposit is £3,125. The optional final payment is £13,400. After 20 months the car is stolen in Manchester and not recovered. The motor insurer agrees a market value of £18,900 and pays it to the finance company.

The settlement figure that week is £23,700. Most of the balloon is still outstanding. An interest rebate reduces the figure, but it does not remove the balloon. The shortfall is £4,800.

Finance GAP written to the settlement figure is aimed at that £4,800, up to the claim limit. Return to invoice, aimed at the £31,250 price, would look at £31,250 minus £18,900, which is £12,350. That is a different, larger top-up. Priya does not get both. If £2,200 of the settlement is negative equity brought over from her previous car, and the policy excludes that, the eligible finance shortfall falls by £2,200.

These pound figures are an illustration so you can see the arithmetic. They are not a quote, a market average, or a claim paid by a named insurer.

Handing the car back is a different right

Ending a PCP early, or handing the car back where the agreement allows it, is explained by MoneyHelper's guide to ending car finance early. Those rights assume there is a car to return or a balance you choose to settle. A write-off means the insurer and the lender are already dealing with a total loss. Voluntary termination does not pay the gap. Neither does the option to hand the car back at the scheduled end.

If you are comparing PCP with a lease, where you never have an option to own the car, use the contract hire guide. The bill there is an early termination charge, not a balloon.

Common questions

Does GAP pay the balloon if I just want to hand the car back on time?
No. GAP responds after the motor insurer has settled a total loss or theft. It does not pay the optional final payment on a PCP that runs to the end with the car still in your hands.
Is the guaranteed minimum future value the same as GAP?
No. The GMFV, or balloon, is the optional amount you can pay if you want to own the car at the end. If you hand the car back in the agreed condition and mileage, you are not billed just because the car is worth less than that balloon. That end-of-contract protection does not pay a write-off shortfall in year two.
Who receives the GAP payment?
The finance company is commonly paid first, up to the settlement figure. Any amount the policy pays above what you owe is dealt with in the wording. Ask the insurer who the cheque or transfer goes to before you spend a payout you have only been told about by phone.
Do I need it if I put down a large deposit?
A large deposit gives you equity, so the shortfall is smaller. It does not remove the balloon from an early settlement. Put a cautious market value next to a written settlement illustration before you decide the cover is unnecessary.
