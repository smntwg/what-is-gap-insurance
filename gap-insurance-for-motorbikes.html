GAP insurance for motorbikes

Home / Guides / GAP insurance for motorbikes

GAP insurance for a motorbike tops up a comprehensive insurer's payment after a total loss or theft, in the same way as for a car. Fewer providers offer it. Theft is a common reason bikes are total losses. An agreed value motor policy is also more common on specialist bikes than on ordinary cars, and it can shrink or remove the gap.

The motor policy still comes first

You cannot ride on GAP insurance alone. The compulsory motor policy is what covers your liability to other people. Third party cover does not pay for your own bike, so a GAP policy that needs a comprehensive settlement will not respond to an at-fault crash. The ABI's description of market value and of GAP is written for vehicles generally, not only cars. Citizens Advice likewise talks about your vehicle's market value on a write-off, and about challenging an offer with prices of similar vehicles.

A theft on hire purchase

Example only

A £2,250 shortfall on a £12,800 motorcycle

Chris buys a new motorcycle in Southampton for £12,800 on hire purchase, with an £800 deposit. Seven months later it is stolen from a station car park and not recovered. The ABI explains that an unrecovered theft is not given a salvage write-off category. The motor insurer agrees a market value of £9,150. The HP settlement figure is £11,400. The shortfall is £2,250.

A motorcycle finance GAP policy is aimed at that £2,250 if bikes are eligible, theft is an insured event, and the claim limit is at least £2,250. The helmet and leathers that were also taken are not part of the £2,250.

If the same bike was a limited-edition model on an agreed value motor policy at £12,500, the motor insurer pays £12,500 where that policy's conditions are met. The £11,400 settlement is cleared and the finance gap disappears. Agreed value has done the job finance GAP would have done. It has to be in force and up to date. It is not, itself, a GAP policy. The distinction is the one in the agreed value comparison.

These pound figures are an illustration so you can see the arithmetic. They are not a quote, a market average, or a claim paid by a named insurer.

Security conditions bite harder on bikes

Bike policies often require a particular lock, a garage overnight, or a tracker. If the motor policy makes that a condition and it was not met, the motor claim can fail. The GAP claim then fails because there is no settlement to top up. Read the schedule before you treat a ground anchor or a disc lock as optional. This is not a universal legal requirement. It is a condition of the policy you bought, and it varies.

Used bikes and the dealer wait

A used bike bought close to the going price, for cash, often has a small gap, in the same way as a used car. A new bike on hire purchase in the first year is the case where the settlement figure and the market value are most likely to part company. If the bike has previously been a category S or N write-off, GOV.UK says insurance can cost more and that you must tell the insurer. A GAP provider may exclude those bikes.

The dealer deferral in ICOBS 6A.1 applies to this sale in the same way as a car. Use it to check that the policy mentions motorcycles in the eligible vehicles, not only in the advert.

Common questions

Are scooters and mopeds included?
Only if that policy's vehicle list includes them. Check the cubic capacity, the value limit and any age limit. A motorcycle policy does not automatically cover a 50cc moped, and a car policy does not cover either.
I changed the exhaust. Does that matter?
Undeclared modifications can give a motor insurer a reason to refuse or reduce a claim. Tell the motor insurer, and make sure the GAP proposal matches. If the motor claim fails, the GAP claim fails with it.
Does GAP cover helmets and leathers?
No. Riding gear and luggage are not part of the bike's shortfall. Some motor policies contribute a small amount towards kit. That is a motor benefit, with its own limit.
Can the dealer sell me bike GAP on the day I collect it?
The ICOBS 6A.1 deferral applies if the firm is selling GAP in connection with the sale of the vehicle. A motorcycle is a vehicle for that purpose. The firm should give you the prescribed information and then wait, unless you yourself use the next-day exception.
