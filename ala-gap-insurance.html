ALA GAP insurance

Home / Guides / ALA GAP insurance

ALA is a UK insurance broker that sells GAP insurance online, alongside car warranties and other motor add-ons. It offers invoice, vehicle replacement, contract hire, agreed value and hire and reward GAP policies. In Auto Express's 2026 test of eight online providers, its quote for the test car was £193.11, the second highest of the eight. This page explains what ALA sells and what has been published about it. It is not an endorsement.

Who ALA is

According to the footer of its website, ALA is ALA IB Limited, trading as ALA Insurance Brokers, registered in England (company number 06833207) with a registered office in Malton, North Yorkshire. It states that it is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority with firm reference number 571109. You can confirm a firm's details on the FCA register before you buy.

ALA is a broker: it arranges the policy, and an insurer carries the risk. ALA says its GAP policies are underwritten by Financial & Legal and Hiscox.

The GAP policies ALA sells

These descriptions and limits are as ALA states them on its GAP page, checked on 21 October 2026. Limits change, so check them on your own quote.

What ALA says about its cover

These are ALA's own claims, reported so you know what to check in the policy documents. We have not tested them.

  • A 99% claims pay-out rate after a total loss.
  • £250 to £500 of motor excess cover as standard, depending on the policy.
  • No admin fees, and free transfers and cancellations.
  • 120 days to make a claim.
  • No market value clauses, and help negotiating the motor insurer's settlement.
  • A price match against comparable online GAP quotes.
  • A typical price range of around £100 to £300 for multi-year cover.

What an independent test found

In Auto Express's 2026 test, eight providers priced the same three-year return-to-invoice cover with a £15,000 maximum claim on a nearly new Ford Puma. ALA's quote was £193.11, rising to £218.10 if paid over ten months, with £250 of excess cover listed. Auto Express liked the simplicity of its site and its clear explanation of two cover types, but said the price was too high to be competitive, and gave it 2.0 stars out of five. The cheapest quote in the same test was £107.35.

That is one car and one cover type. ALA's wider product range, such as agreed value cover with no age or mileage limit, may suit a car other policies will not accept, which a single price test does not measure. See the full test results and comparison checks.

Example only

How a 'higher of invoice or finance' policy pays

Jas buys a car for £22,000 on hire purchase. A year later it is written off. The motor insurer pays the market value of £15,500. The finance settlement on that day is £17,200.

A policy that tops up to the higher of the invoice price or the finance settlement works from £22,000, so the shortfall it would aim to cover is £6,500, subject to the claim limit. A finance-only policy would cover £1,700. ALA describes its Back to Invoice Plus policy with this 'higher of' calculation, so check which figure any policy you are offered works from.

These pound figures are an illustration so you can see the arithmetic. They are not a quote, a market average, or a claim paid by a named insurer.

Before you buy from ALA or anyone else

  1. Pick the cover type that matches your finance or ownership: the cover types are compared here.
  2. Get at least one other quote for the same cover type, claim limit and term.
  3. Check the eligibility limits and the claim time limit in the policy wording.
  4. Confirm the firm on the FCA register and keep your documents. You normally have 14 days to cancel.

Common questions

Who is ALA GAP insurance with?
ALA is the broker. Its website says its GAP policies are underwritten by Financial & Legal and Hiscox. Your policy documents will name the insurer for your policy.
Is ALA regulated by the FCA?
ALA's website says ALA IB Limited, trading as ALA Insurance Brokers, is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority, firm reference 571109. You can check that number on the FCA register.
How much does ALA GAP insurance cost?
It depends on the car and the cover. ALA's own guide gives a typical range of around £100 to £300 for multi-year cover. In Auto Express's 2026 like-for-like test, ALA quoted £193.11 for three years of return-to-invoice cover on a nearly new Ford Puma, or £218.10 over ten months.
Can I get ALA GAP for a car I bought months ago?
Possibly. ALA says its Back to Invoice Plus policy is for cars bought within the last 180 days, or 365 days where new car replacement applies for the first year, and its contract hire cover is for vehicles delivered within the last 365 days. Check the current eligibility when you quote.
