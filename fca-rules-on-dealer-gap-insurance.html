FCA rules on dealer GAP insurance

Home / Guides / FCA rules on dealer GAP insurance

If a firm sells you GAP insurance in connection with selling you the vehicle, FCA rules in ICOBS 6A.1 make it give you set information and then wait before the sale is completed. Separate fair value rules are why the FCA asked firms to pause GAP sales in 2024 until they could show customers were getting a fairer deal. The rules do not ban dealer GAP, and they do not set a maximum price in pounds.

Which sales the deferral covers

ICOBS 6A.1.1 applies to a firm that sells a GAP contract in connection with the sale of a vehicle by that firm, or by a person connected to the firm. A connection includes acting as an introducer or appointed representative, or another relevant business relationship. The FCA also says there is a sufficient connection if GAP is sold with other goods, for example a credit agreement. A standalone insurer you approach yourself, with no link to the vehicle sale, is outside that section.

The rules have applied since 1 September 2015. They came out of a competition remedy. In CP14/29 the FCA said add-on prices could be twice as expensive as broadly equivalent standalone cover, and that 83% of add-on GAP buyers in its market study did not consider another policy, compared with 9% of standalone buyers.

The information you should be given

Before the contract is concluded, ICOBS 6A.1.4 says the firm must give you all of the following, clearly, on paper or another durable medium, and identifiable as key information:

  • The total premium of the GAP contract, separate from any other prices.
  • The significant features and benefits, the significant and unusual exclusions or limitations, and where to find them in the policy.
  • That GAP contracts are sold by other distributors, whether or not this sale is tied to vehicle finance.
  • How long the policy lasts.
  • Whether the GAP contract is optional or compulsory.
  • When the firm is allowed to conclude the contract.
  • The date that information was provided.

The deferral, with dates

ICOBS 6A.1.6 says the firm cannot conclude the contract until at least two clear days have passed since it gave you that information. ICOBS 6A.1.7 is the exception. The firm can conclude it the day after giving you the information if you initiate that conclusion, you consent to the shorter wait, and you confirm that you understand the usual restriction. The firm is not supposed to talk you into using the exception as if it were the normal path.

Example only

Information on Monday 1 June

A dealer gives you the prescribed GAP information on Monday 1 June. The two clear days are Tuesday 2 June and Wednesday 3 June. The firm should not complete the sale before Thursday 4 June.

If you contact the dealer yourself on Tuesday 2 June, agree to the shorter wait, and confirm you understand that the firm would normally have to wait those two clear days, ICOBS 6A.1.7 allows the firm to conclude the contract that Tuesday. A sale finished on the Monday afternoon, the same day as the information, does not fit either rule.

This is an illustration of the calendar, not a story about a particular dealer. The same count applies whatever the premium is.

These pound figures are an illustration so you can see the arithmetic. They are not a quote, a market average, or a claim paid by a named insurer.

The FCA's July 2018 evaluation looked at what the 2015 rules changed. Shopping around more than doubled. Add-on GAP sales were 16% to 23% lower than they would have been without the intervention. Standalone sales rose from 6% to 8% of all GAP sales. The FCA said the shift towards the standalone market was smaller than it had expected before the rules began.

Fair value, the 2024 pause, and the restart

Information rules and price are different problems. On 9 February 2024 the FCA said multiple insurers had agreed to pause GAP sales after it was not satisfied they were providing fair value. The press release said that only 6% of the amount customers pay in premiums for GAP insurance was paid out in claims, and that the FCA had seen examples of firms paying out 70% of the value of premiums in commission. It also pointed back to the 2014 market study, which found that only 10% of premiums were paid out in claims on average between 2008 and 2012. The FCA had written to manufacturers in September 2023 asking them to prove customers were getting a fair deal.

On 24 May 2024 the FCA said several firms could start selling again, with materially lower commission. The firms named at that date were Fortegra Europe Insurance Company Ltd, Motors Insurance Company Ltd, Amtrust Europe Ltd, and Financial & Legal Insurance Company Limited. The FCA said that decision reflected its assessment at a point in time, and that firms still have to keep checking fair value. Naming them here is not a recommendation. A November 2024 FCA note on a freedom of information request repeats the same 6% and 70% picture and ties the fair value assessment to the Consumer Duty at PRIN 2A.4.

What these rules do not do

  • They do not make GAP compulsory, and they do not ban it.
  • They do not cap the premium at a particular price.
  • They do not force you to buy from a standalone website. They give you time and information so you can.
  • They do not apply the dealer deferral to every insurer in the country.
  • They do not mean a customer who paid a high price is automatically entitled to a refund. A sale that ignored the information or the wait is a complaint. How to raise one is in the mis-selling guide.

Check the seller on the Financial Services Register before you pay. If you are deciding whether to use the pause to buy elsewhere, the practical timing is in can I buy GAP after buying the car.

Common questions

Does the two-clear-day wait apply if I buy from a website a week later?
Not under ICOBS 6A.1, if that firm is not selling the GAP contract in connection with its own sale of the vehicle, or a connected person's sale. The deferral is a rule for the add-on sale. A later standalone purchase has the provider's own time limits instead.
Can a dealer hide the GAP premium inside the monthly payment?
The rules say the total premium must be given separately from any other prices, on paper or another durable medium, and drawn to your attention as key information. A blended monthly figure with no separate premium does not do that.
Is the 6% claims figure my chance of claiming?
No. It is a market-wide comparison, from the FCA's value measures data, of GAP claims paid against premiums paid. The FCA cited the 2022 data when it challenged firms in 2024. Your own chance depends on the car, how it is used, and whether it is stolen or written off.
Where do I check a firm is allowed to sell insurance?
On the Financial Services Register. An appointed representative is listed against the authorised firm that is responsible for it. A firm that does not appear needs a better explanation before you pay it.

Related guides

Dealer vs online GAP insurance

The same shortfall can be insured in the showroom or later online. The sale, and often the price, is different.

GAP insurance mis-selling

A sale that skips the FCA information rules is a complaint. A price you later regret is not automatically the same thing.

Can I buy GAP insurance after buying the car?

Usually yes, inside that provider's time limit, and only before a loss. There is no single legal deadline.

Cancelling GAP insurance and refunds

Most policies can be cancelled. The 14-day right and a later pro-rata refund are not the same thing.
